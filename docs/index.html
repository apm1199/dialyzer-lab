MODEL EQUATIONS & SCOPE

A small model.
A precise interpretation.

01 / Effective membrane transport

The effective diffusivity Dapp(Qd) is represented by a shape-preserving cubic Hermite correlation over 0–133.33 mL/min. Its nodes are 0, 17.5, 41.67, 66.67 and 133.33 mL/min; the final derivative equals the last secant slope.

The zero node is a modelling constraint, not an experimental measurement. Water and blood use the same effective membrane correlation. Fluid-specific transport is represented through the overall equivalent factor.

02 / Reduction to a rapid exchanger

The cylindrical length is ℓc = ri ln(ro/ri), and the inner area is A = 2πri L N. The nominal membrane conductance is A Dapp / ℓc. An equivalent factor η maps this conductance to the overall apparent K₀A.

K₀A(Qd) = η(Qd) A Dapp(Qd) / ℓc

At each calibration condition, η is identified by inverting the countercurrent clearance relation. Its values are interpolated with PCHIP. Below the first positive calibration point, η is held constant: below 17.5 mL/min for water and 41.67 mL/min for blood.

η represents the combined unresolved fluid-side and internal-convection response. It may exceed one and is not a membrane diffusivity or a physical film coefficient. This additional reduction has its own numerical checks; it does not inherit CFD validation automatically.

03 / From K₀A to clearance

Define Qmin = min(Qw,Qd), Qmax = max(Qw,Qd), Cr = Qmin/Qmax and NTU = K₀A/Qmin. The countercurrent exchanger effectiveness is:

ε = [1 − exp(−NTU(1 − Cr))] / [1 − Cr exp(−NTU(1 − Cr))]
Kwater = Qmin ε

For Qw = Qd = Q, Kwater = Q K₀A/(Q + K₀A). At Qd = 0, clearance and removal are zero and there is no dialysate effluent concentration. This is a steady zero-net-UF limit; it is not an HF simulation.

04 / Blood and concentration balances

Qw = fw Qb, with fw = (1 − Hct)fw,p + Hct fw,e = 0.8565 for the default blood preset. Cw,in = Cbulk,in/fw. For solute-free dialysate and zero net UF:

ṁ = Kwater Cw,in
Cw,out = Cw,in(1 − Kwater/Qw)
Cd,out = Kwater Cw,in/Qd
Kbulk = Kwater/fw

The blood preset combines Carreau–Yasuda rheology and an aqueous-phase transport description in its calibration dataset. No velocity, shear-rate or local Jv field is solved. Predictions below 41.67 mL/min are not validated blood results.

05 / Data and applicability

Model parameters and datasets are informed by literature and numerical studies. Default datasets support the stated filter geometry and operating conditions; user-imported datasets retain their declared origin.

The default blood dataset consists of numerical predictions, not experimental blood measurements. Geometry, Qb, fluid properties and net UF stay fixed per preset. Geometry and permeability sensitivity require a coupled physical model or additional data.
